Tutorials
----------

On the `GROMACS tutorial page <http:\\tutorials.gromacs.org>`_  you find a
collection of training resource and free online GROMACS tutorials, provided as
interactive Jupyter notebooks. More on the GROMACS tutorial 
`here <https://bioexcel.eu/webinar-the-gromacs-tutorial-suite-from-basic-to-advanced-applications-2025-12-2/>`_
